## Description

The Netherlands' NIS2 transposition law, the Cyberbeveiligingswet, enters into force August 15, 2026, following Dutch Senate approval on July 7, 2026. This isn't a certificate-specific law, but it changes what enterprise cert teams with Dutch operations need to document for compliance.

🔑 Key Points:
- Cyberbeveiligingswet (NIS2) and Wet weerbaarheid kritieke entiteiten (CER) approved by Dutch Senate July 7, 2026
- Cyberbeveiligingswet enters into force August 15, 2026, establishing a three-tier regulatory regime
- EU Commission referred Netherlands, Ireland, Spain, and France to the CJEU on July 8, 2026 over NIS2 non-transposition
- Certificate-related incidents (key compromise, mis-issuance, revocation failures, expiry outages) increasingly fall under NIS2 incident-reporting scope
- No Dutch entities or operations? No action needed from this specific law
