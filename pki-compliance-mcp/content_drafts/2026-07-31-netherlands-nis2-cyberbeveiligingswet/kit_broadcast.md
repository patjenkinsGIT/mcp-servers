## Subject

Netherlands NIS2 law goes live August 15, 2026

## Preview text

Dutch Senate approved it July 7 - here's what your cert team needs to check

## Body

Hey,

The Netherlands' NIS2 transposition law — the Cyberbeveiligingswet — enters into force on August 15, 2026. The Dutch Senate approved it on July 7, 2026, along with the CER transposition (Wet weerbaarheid kritieke entiteiten).

**Who's affected:** Dutch entities classified as essential or important under NIS2's new three-tier regime. This is a broad cybersecurity/incident-reporting law, not a certificate-specific one. **If you have no Dutch operations, you can ignore this one** — no action needed.

If you *do* have Dutch entities in scope, here's why it matters to a cert team specifically: NIS2's incident-reporting obligations increasingly capture the kind of things your cert inventory already tracks — key compromise, mis-issuance, revocation failures, expiry-driven outages. Regulators will expect that evidence trail.

**Key dates:**
- July 7, 2026 — Senate approves Cyberbeveiligingswet + CER law
- July 8, 2026 — EU Commission refers NL (with Ireland, Spain, France) to CJEU over NIS2 non-transposition
- August 15, 2026 — Cyberbeveiligingswet enters into force

**What to do this week:**
1. Confirm whether any Dutch entity/subsidiary falls into NIS2's essential or important tier.
2. If yes, check that cert-related incidents are explicit categories in your NIS2 incident-reporting workflow.
3. Pull your cert inventory, key-management policy docs, and incident logs into one place — this is your evidence trail if asked.
4. Keep an eye on whether formal notification to the EU Commission has been lodged; it hadn't been as of the Senate vote.

I'm keeping the live tracker updated as more member states move through their own transpositions: https://fixmycert.com/compliance
