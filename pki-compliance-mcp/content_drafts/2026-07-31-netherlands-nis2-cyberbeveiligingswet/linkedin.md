The Netherlands' NIS2 transposition law — the Cyberbeveiligingswet — enters into force on August 15, 2026.

The Dutch Senate approved it on July 7, 2026, alongside the CER transposition (Wet weerbaarheid kritieke entiteiten). It sets up a three-tier regulatory regime for in-scope entities.

This isn't a certificate-specific law. But if you operate Dutch entities classified as essential or important under NIS2, your incident-reporting process now needs to treat certificate-related incidents — key compromise, mis-issuance, revocation failures, expiry outages — as reportable events. Your cert inventory and incident logs are compliance evidence now, not just ops hygiene.

Worth noting: the EU Commission referred the Netherlands (with Ireland, Spain, France) to the CJEU on July 8, 2026 over NIS2 non-transposition — one day after the Senate vote. How that resolves is a legal detail to watch, not something to guess at.

If you have no Dutch operations, this one doesn't apply to you directly.
